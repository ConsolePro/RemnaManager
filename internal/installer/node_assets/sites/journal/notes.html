Journal / 01

A better loaf

Cooking is a daily practice. We value simple methods, ingredients in season, and recipes that leave room for your own judgement.

Start by setting aside the idea of a perfect result. Notice what is already working and make one small change at a time. Keeping a record makes it easier to see which details matter and which can be left alone.

The value of a practice is in returning to it. There is always another variation to try, another season to observe, or another way to share what you have learned.
