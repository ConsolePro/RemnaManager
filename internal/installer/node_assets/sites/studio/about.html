Our approach

Paying attention.

Thoughtful spaces begin with observation. We look at the changing light, the objects people choose to keep, and the paths they take through a room.

This is a small independent collection of ideas, observations and work in progress. The aim is to make room for useful questions and to share the discoveries that follow.

Close at hand

A well-used notebook, an unfamiliar route, a conversation that continues over dinner. Much of the most interesting work begins with these ordinary encounters.
